## 8 · The converter fix (ruling B7)

The white, untextured slab at the top of the entrance stairs (Task 9's `entrance.png`) was the
converter's: `FindSiblingCmdl` took the first `<stem>_*.cmdl` in ordinal order, so
`arena_01_entrance_skirt.cmsh` used skirt_02's cmdl (one of its four materials mapped) instead of its own
`arena_01_entrance_skirt_ec63e197.cmdl`. The fix (`495f38c`) prefers a suffix of exactly 8 hex digits and
falls back to the first match (`docs/notes/aqua-core-api.md` §10.1). Over the 22,012 cmsh it changes
**10 picks** and loses none; the 10 were rebaked into the view only — 8 glbs changed,
`cathedral_ext_fur` and `hall_16_skirts` re-convert byte-identical; base-colour-textured materials over
the 10 went from 5 to 16 of 16. **The base `out/library` keeps the old glbs until S9's merge** (other base
consumers, e.g. the entrance skirt in the valus/encounter manifests, keep the white materials until
then). The xUnit suite pins the skirt's pick and a `_group` owner's first-match fallback.
