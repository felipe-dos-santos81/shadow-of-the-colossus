# S8 "Agro" Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add the plain in front of the shrine as a third stage reached through the shrine's door, Agro standing on it, and riding — whistle, mount, walk/trot/gallop by kicks, steer, rein in, stop, back up, dismount — on the game's own clips, speeds and sounds.

**Architecture:** An RE/data gate (W2) places Agro, the doors and the probes and measures the clips' root travel; the probe composes `plain.manifest` with new `horse` and `backdrop` records (W3); the converter bakes Agro's and Wander's ride clips and the S8 audio slice (W4); the app gains a hole-refusing ground, a local-pose blend, the records, a pure riding module, a pure door machine, an explicit `Plain` stage with a horse rig and a bone-attached rider, and the S8 cue table (W1); `--s8-selftest`, `--s8-e2e` and `scripts/s8-acceptance.sh` prove it after the frozen S7 chain.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, SDL3/SDL_GPU/Metal, doctest, .NET 10 (BpConvert), Python 3 (stdlib), Ghidra 12.1.4 + Ghidra-MCP, REA 6.3.0.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-s8-agro-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Apple Silicon arm64, C++20, SDL3/SDL_GPU/Metal; no new runtime dependency; windowed, the cursor never captured.
- Offline-first; the game tree is read-only; assets/derived files gitignored, never committed; RE notes carry addresses/counts/sizes, no game content.
- Frozen and invoked unchanged: `scripts/s7-acceptance.sh` … `s3-acceptance.sh`, `s5_map.json`, `s6_map.json`, the arena/encounter/shrine manifests, and the output of `--selftest`, `--s3`…`--s7-selftest` and `--s7-e2e`.
- No frozen selftest or the S7 e2e ever enters the plain, steps the riding or door machines, or plays an S8 cue.
- `out/library` is shared by every branch and worktree: Wander's 17 clips keep their indices (26 ride clips appended); Agro keeps `horse_a_add_horse_kick` at index 0 (19 riding clips appended, 20 total); the exact Agro pin is relaxed on `main` before the bake, landing on `main` only with the user's OK.
- Every viewer/acceptance run under `caffeinate -dimsu`; never two acceptances concurrently; never a bake while an acceptance runs; scripts bash 3.2; tests fail fast when `SOTC_GAME_ROOT`/`SOTC_LIB` are unset.
- Authored values (Agro's start, the door boxes/spawns, yaw rates, camera distances, whistle ranges, blend length, rein hold) carry `source: authored` and are labelled; S8 cue rows are `method: audition`.
- ≤ 4 concurrent agents; W1 (app) and W2 (RE) never share files — findings cross as `docs/notes/s8-recon.md` and `tools/re/s8/placements.json`.

## Review Focus

1. **Dismount request at a gallop, mount request beside a cliff.** Dismount must be refused above Walk (rein first) and the dismount side must have walkable ground or the other side is used, else refused. Test: `test_ride.cpp` "dismount refused at trot/gallop", "dismount picks the walkable side", "dismount refused with no walkable side".
2. **`R` while mounted, mid-mount, mid-door-fade or on the plain.** The arena must come back at its opening state with the rider detached and both machines idle. Test: `test_ride.cpp`/`test_door.cpp` reset cases; Task 9's `s8-reset` line after an `R` injected while mounted at a gallop.
3. **Kick spam and kicks at top speed.** Three kicks inside 1.0 s start a 3.0 s cooldown; a kick at ≥ 0.9 × max speed is refused with a neigh; the gait never exceeds Gallop. Test: `test_ride.cpp` kick-buffer cases.
4. **Riding off the plateau or into the pond.** The cliff edge refuses the move (no ground, drop > 1.5 m or rise > 1.0 m); the pond is walkable; Agro on a whistle approach obeys the same rule and warps after 10 s without progress to a target that has ground. Test: `test_heightmap.cpp` refuseHoles cases; `test_ride.cpp` whistle-stuck case; Task 9's `s8-edge` line.
5. **Frozen outputs after the anim split and the stage-loop changes.** Every frozen selftest line and still must be unchanged. Test: `test_anim.cpp` "local pose → palette equals animation_pose" over the existing fixtures; the frozen S7 chain in Tasks 4, 7 and 10.
