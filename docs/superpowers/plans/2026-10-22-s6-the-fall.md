# S6 "The fall" Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Give Valus's defeat the game's payoff — the death motion, the black tendrils reconstructed from the shipped `.cptx`/veins assets and the binary's 201-segment geometry, the collapse to the dead body, a cinematic camera, the death roar and club whoosh, and a stable encounter end.

**Architecture:** A pure `death.h/.cpp` phase machine drives the sequence; a new `EffectRenderer` adds the first world-space blended ribbon/billboard pass to the SDL_GPU renderer; the converter gains an emissive texture slot for the dead body's veins layer; the S5 audio slice grows by two audition-verified cues in a new `s6_map.json`; the app wires the defeat, camera, corpse, input lock and audio, and proves it with `--s6-selftest --s6-stills` and `scripts/s6-acceptance.sh`.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, SDL3/SDL_GPU/Metal, .NET 10, Python 3, the existing BpConvert converter and S5 audio tools, Ghidra/REA evidence.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-s6-the-fall-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Apple Silicon arm64, C++20, SDL3/SDL_GPU/Metal; no new runtime dependency; the viewer stays windowed and never captures the cursor.
- Static-only RE; the game tree is read-only; converted assets, glbs, PNGs and audio are gitignored and never committed.
- S5 audio contract unchanged: 48 kHz float32 WAVs, the `audio.manifest` key=value lines, `gain_db` provenance-only, the S5 decoder/gate untouched by the runtime.
- The frozen `scripts/s5-acceptance.sh`, `s42-acceptance.sh`, `s41-acceptance.sh`, `s4-acceptance.sh`, `s3-acceptance.sh` are invoked unchanged; S6 adds `s6-*` lines/stills only.
- Every viewer/acceptance run under `caffeinate -dimsu`; never two acceptances concurrently.
- Bash 3.2; tests fail fast when `SOTC_GAME_ROOT`/`SOTC_LIB` are unset; no silent skips.
- The tendril effect is a **labeled reconstruction**: the original textures/emitters/segment geometry, the authored curl/turbulence and camera; the `.csdr` sim and type-36 camera clips are not ported.
- The player's death (S4.2) and Valus's death are separate terminations; the death sequence runs only on `encounter.defeated`; `R`/`M`/`Escape` always work.

## Review Focus

1. **The player dead on the same frame Valus is defeated.** The sequence must not run over the player's death; `apply_combat_events` must keep the player's death terminal and the Valus defeat its own transition. Test: `test_death.cpp` — `step_death(Idle, dt, false)` stays Idle; the Task 5 `s6-status` keeps `player_dead` and `defeated` separate.
2. **`R` pressed mid-cinematic.** The reset must restore the opening state (the phase Idle, the corpse hidden, the lock off, the camera follow, Valus 100, HP 100) with no stuck lock or orphan corpse. Test: `test_death.cpp` reset case + the Task 5 `s6-reset`/`s6-status`.
3. **A missing effect texture or death clip.** The engine must fail loudly at load, not draw a blank or a degenerate ribbon. Test: the Task 0 gate `--check`; the Task 4 `EffectRenderer::create` throws on a missing PNG.
4. **A tendril root with no hit point.** A fresh defeat or a reset replay must fall back to the weakpoint shell and never produce a zero-length/NaN ribbon. Test: `test_effect.cpp` — zero roots yields zero vertices; a root at the origin yields finite vertices.
5. **The S5 manifest (8) vs the S6 manifest (10).** The frozen S5 acceptance must still see exactly 8 entries; the S6 converter runs after it and the S6 selftest loads 10. Test: the Task 6 acceptance order asserts both counts.
6. **The windowed/cursor invariant during the cinematic.** The S6 selftest asserts `s1-status windowed=1 relative_mouse=0 mouse_auto_capture=0`; the cinematic must not capture the mouse.
7. **The effect pass's frame cost.** The 240-frame run at the tendril phase must stay within the S4.2 class (≤ 16.7 ms/frame). Test: the Task 6 perf check.
